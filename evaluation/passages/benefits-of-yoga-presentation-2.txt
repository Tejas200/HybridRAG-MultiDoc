•Benefits of Yoga: A Path to Healthy Body and Peaceful Mind •Yoga is an ancient practice that originated in India and is now followed all over the world. In today’s fast-paced lifestyle, stress, anxiety, and health problems have become very common. Practicing yoga regularly can help people achieve physical fitness, mental clarity, and emotional balance. The benefits of yoga go beyond simple exercise, making it a complete lifestyle practice.

•Another important aspect of the benefits of yoga is better mental health. Yoga includes breathing techniques and meditation that calm the mind and reduce stress. People who practice yoga daily experience lower levels of anxiety and depression. It improves concentration, memory, and mental focus, which is helpful for students and working professionals alike.
