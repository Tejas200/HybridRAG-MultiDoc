=== amys-gravity:0005:1 | page 5 | section | section: 'Gravity and Antigravity - Definitions' | words 75 ===
- Gravity: a fundamental physical force that is responsible for interactions which occur because of mass between particles, between aggregations of matter (such as stars and planets), and between particles (such as photons) and aggregations of matter, that is 10-39 times the strength of the strong force, and that extends over infinite distances but is dominant over macroscopic distances especially between aggregations of matter - Antigravity: Reducing, canceling, or protecting against the effect of gravity

=== amys-gravity:0006:1 | page 6 | section | section: 'Gravity - The Basics' | words 63 ===
